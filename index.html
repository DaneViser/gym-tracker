Rules to train by
  • Start the new exercises light for 2–3 weeks. Carries and sandbag work feel easy until the next day.
  • Add a little weight or 1 rep each week while form stays clean.
  • Every 4–6 weeks, take an easy week at about 60% effort.
  • Short on time? Cut arm work first, never the carries or core.
  • Brace before every lift, keep the load close, and turn your feet instead of twisting under load.
  • Protein: about 1.6 g per kg of bodyweight a day. Sleep matters.

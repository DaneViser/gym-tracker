Warm-up: 5 min mobility
  • 90/90 hip switches: 10 per side
  • Deep squat hold: 1–2 min total, holding onto something is fine
  • Couch stretch: 45 s per side
  • Thoracic rotations: 8 per side
  • Dead hang: 2 × 20–30 s (good before upper days too)
Rules to train by
  • Start new exercises light for 2–3 weeks.
  • Add a little weight or 1 rep each week while form stays clean.
  • Every 4–6 weeks, take an easy week at about 60% effort.
  • Short on time? Cut arm work first, never the carries or core.
  • Brace before every lift, keep the load close, and turn your feet instead of twisting under load.
  • Protein: about 1.6 g per kg of bodyweight a day. Sleep matters.

How it works

Six things to know:

  • 📅One card per workoutToday's workout is highlighted orange. Tap a card to open it.
  • ✅Tick as you goTick every exercise and the day completes itself, or tap the big box on the right.
  • 🏋️Write your weightsNext week the box shows last week's weight in grey, so you know what to beat.
  • ✏️Make it yoursTap Edit inside a workout to change exercises and days, or + Add workout for a new one.
  • ⏱️Rest timer & weeksRest timer is at the bottom. Swipe left or right to change week.
  • 💾Saved on this phoneUse Back up data at the bottom now and then, in case you clear your browser.
